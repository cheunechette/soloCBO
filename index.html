Who We Are

Dedicated to Grassroots Health Equity

Founded locally to bridge the gap between rural communities and essential medical services. We work directly with village health teams (VHTs) and health centers.

Mobile Health Clinics

Deploying volunteer doctors, nurses, and lab technicians directly into remote areas to conduct free medical screenings and dispensations.

Maternal & Infant Care

Providing clean birth kits, antenatal screenings, and essential nutritional support to expectant mothers in underprivileged sub-counties.

Disease Prevention

Conducting sensitization campaigns for malaria prevention, HIV testing & counseling, and hygiene education in primary schools.
